A bioinformatics researcher and developer connecting biological questions with analysis design and interpretation. My work extends from microbes to humans, integrating diverse analytical approaches and building research pipelines and databases. AI-powered coding accelerates my research and amplifies my ability to automate workflows and develop analytical tools.

## At a glance

- **6+ years** in applied bioinformatics and genomics operations
- Hands-on analysis and service leadership across microbial genomics, microbiome and transcriptome work
- **7 SCI(E) publications** spanning NGS pathogen panels and experimental biomedicine
- **2 peer-reviewed NGS panel papers and 1 joint patent** connected to foodborne-pathogen detection

## Research experience

### MicrobiotiX · July 2026–present

Analyze bacterial and phage genomes and design host-recognition protein candidates. Develop phage research databases and prediction tools; contribute to cocktail selection software and provide analysis technical support.

### Wittgenbio · July–October 2026

Built a CRC single-cell atlas, annotated cell types and analyzed expression of target candidates.

### Sanigen · March 2020–November 2025

Managed NGS analysis services and the bioinformatics team; performed genomics, microbiome and transcriptome analyses. Automated analysis workflows and reports and contributed to marker and panel research. Provided technical support to clients and internal researchers and operated analysis infrastructure. Contributed to the technology-special listing task force and prepared technical-evaluation and listing-review materials.

The case describes RNA-level target evidence and its interpretation limits without exposing private datasets or internal identifiers.
